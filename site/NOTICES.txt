The scene is the pruned full_7k export with view-independent (zero-order) colour.
The preview is a recorded browser screenshot. The viewer interface is adapted
for publication; no original image, lidar scan, training checkpoint or full model
is distributed by this website. The MIT code licence does not cover these data.

Argoverse 2: B. Wilson et al., Argoverse 2: Next Generation Datasets for
Self-Driving Perception and Forecasting, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks, 2021.
Method: B. Kerbl et al., 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field
Rendering, SIGGRAPH 2023. Training implementation: gsplat (Apache-2.0).

Viewer code: (c) 2026 Wenjie Luo, MIT. See LICENSE-code.txt.
Renderer: @mkkellogg/gaussian-splats-3d 0.4.7, MIT. See vendor/LICENSE-gaussian.txt.
Three.js: three 0.160.0, MIT. See vendor/LICENSE-three.txt.
The dependencies are pinned and served from this same website, including the
renderer source map. They are not fetched from a CDN at runtime.

This is one short, single-camera clip. It has no dynamic-object model, no
off-path RGB ground truth and no general driving-scene or phone-performance
guarantee. Visible floaters, gaps and blur remain part of the published result.
